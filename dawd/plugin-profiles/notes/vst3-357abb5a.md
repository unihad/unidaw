Diva (u-he) — analog-modelled synth (VCO/filter models of classic polysynths), very warm, CPU-heavy.

- Presets are .h2p files in categories (1 BASS, 2 LEAD, 3 POLY SYNTH, 4 DREAM SYNTH, 5 PERCUSSIVE, 6 RHYTHMIC, 7 EFFECTS, 8 TEMPLATES, THIRD PARTY): listPresets {plugin, query} → loadPreset. Its 128 VST3 programs are unnamed and are not listed.
- 'Voices' and 'Mode' (poly/mono/duo) shape the playing; 'Transpose' is in semitones.
- Parameters carry texts — set by text where possible.
