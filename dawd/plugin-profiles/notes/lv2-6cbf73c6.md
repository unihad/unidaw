MDA Combo — amp and speaker cabinet simulator (with drive).

- Processes MONO by default: Stereo = Off sums the output to mono (measured correlation 1 on stereo noise); switch Stereo on for a stereo source.
- Model picks the speaker: D.I. (flat), speaker simulator, small radio, small combo close/far mic, large stack front/side mic (scooped mids) — the strongest change in tone (brightness, lows and highs).
- Drive = amount of clipping, Bias = asymmetric clipping (rougher, "gated"); with the test signal Drive changed the level a little, Bias nothing measurable — judge by ear (describeTimbre roughness). HPF Freq / HPF Reso cut the lows.
- Put time effects (delay, reverb) after it. As a cabinet only (no drive) after MDA Overdrive or Surge XT Effects distortion.
