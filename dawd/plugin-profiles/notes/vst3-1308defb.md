Dragonfly Room Reverb — small to medium room; adds space and glue without a long tail.

- Own mix: Dry Level 2, Early Level, Late Level (0–100 %); the first "Dry level"/"Wet Level" are dawd's wrapper levels.
- Size (m, 8–32), Decay (s, keep under ~1.5 s for a room), Predelay (ms).
- Early Damp / Late Damp (Hz) darken reflections and tail; Low Boost + Boost Freq thicken the room, Low Cut removes rumble.
- Good for drums and guitars; for vocals use Plate, for big spaces Hall.
