Dragonfly Hall Reverb — natural concert-hall reverb (Freeverb3-based), the default choice for a big, smooth space.

- The first "Dry level"/"Wet Level" are dawd's wrapper levels; the plugin's own mix is Dry Level 2, Early Level, Late Level (0–100 %). On a parallel wet path (wet edge in the routing matrix) set Dry Level 2 = 0.
- Size (m, 10–60) and Decay (s) set the hall; Predelay (ms, 0–100) keeps vocals/snares in front — 20–40 ms is typical.
- Low Cut / High Cut (Hz) shape the tail: Low Cut 100–200 keeps the low end clean, High Cut 4–8 kHz for a darker hall.
- Low Mult / High Mult scale the decay of lows/highs (below 1 = shorter); Modulation, Spin, Wander add movement to the tail.
