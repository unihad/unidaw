MDA JX10 — simple analog-style poly synth (Roland JX-10 inspired), 52 presets (listPresets).

- Oscillators: OSC Mix (two oscillators), OSC Tune / OSC Fine (detune), Noise, Octave; Glide (Gld Rate/Gld Bend) for portamento.
- Filter: VCF Freq, VCF Reso, VCF Env (envelope amount), VCF LFO, VCF Vel; filter envelope VCF Att/Dec/Sus/Rel, amp envelope ENV Att/Dec/Sus/Rel.
- All parameters 0–1 without units — start from a preset (basses, pads, leads) and adjust filter and envelopes.
