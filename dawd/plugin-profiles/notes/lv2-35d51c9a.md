Stereo Balance Control (x42) — utility: trim, L/R balance, polarity, delay per channel, channel routing.

- Phase Invert Left / Right flips the polarity of one side: on a mono source L = −R, which the analysis measures as pure side (and a mono sum cancels). Use it to fix a flipped mic, not as an effect.
- Delay Left / Delay Right are in samples (at 44.1 kHz 441 samples = 10 ms): delaying one side of a mono part widens it (Haas delay; measured stereo width −40 → 0 dB). Check the mono sum afterwards (analyze).
- Balance L/R with Gain Mode (Balance, Unity Gain, Seesaw – Equal Power); Trim/Gain in dB.
- Channel Assignment: straight, left or right channel only, swap, downmix to mono.
