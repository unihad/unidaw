MDA ePiano — Rhodes-like electric piano, few controls, 5 presets (listPresets).

- Envelope Decay / Envelope Release (length of notes), Hardness (attack bite), Treble Boost (brightness), Velocity Sense.
- Modulation + LFO Rate = tremolo/auto-pan; Stereo Width; Overdrive for a gritty Rhodes.
- All parameters are 0–1 without units; the program presets are the quickest start.
