Dragonfly Plate Reverb — plate reverb: bright, dense, no early reflections; classic on vocals and snare.

- Own mix: Dry Level 2 / Wet Level 2 (0–100 %); the first "Dry level"/"Wet level" are dawd's wrapper levels.
- Algorithm: Simple, Nested, Tank (Tank = densest, smoothest).
- Decay (s, 1–2.5 for vocals), Predelay (ms), Dampen (Hz, lower = darker), Low Cut / High Cut.
