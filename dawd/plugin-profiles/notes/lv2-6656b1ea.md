x42-comp — simple stereo compressor with few controls.

- Threshold (dB, -50…-10), Ratio (0–1, higher = stronger), Attack Time / Release Time (s), Input Gain (dB, makes up level before the compressor).
- Hold On freezes the gain reduction — leave Off.
- Quick glue on buses or vocals; for detailed control (knee, lookahead, sidechain filter) use LSP Compressor.
