MDA DubDelay — dub-style feedback delay with filtered, saturating repeats.

- Delay (time, 0–1, not tempo-synced), Feedback (high values self-oscillate — watch the level), Fb Tone (darker/brighter repeats).
- LFO Depth / LFO Rate wobble the delay time (tape feel); FX Mix, Output.
- For tempo-synced echoes use the builtin Delay or Surge XT Effects (Delay).
