# Contributing to unidaw

Thank you for your interest. unidaw is made by a one-person company, so the
rules are short.

## Issues: welcome

- **Bug reports** are welcome. Please use the bug report form and include
  the unidaw version, your Linux distribution and the steps to repeat the
  bug.
- **Ideas and questions** are welcome as issues, too.
- There is **no support** and **no promise of a reply**. An issue may stay
  open without an answer.
- Security problems: do not open a public issue. See
  [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

## Pull requests: not accepted

Pull requests from outside are **not accepted** and will be closed
without review. This is not about the quality of your work: one person
can not review, maintain and take legal responsibility for outside code.

If you found a fix, describe it in an issue. You may also keep your own
fork; the license (AGPL-3.0-only, see [LICENSE](LICENSE)) allows that.
